My work

Turning ideas into practice.

My work sits across the intersection of lived experience, business, property, investment and public policy.

The common thread is a belief that complex challenges require practical, sustainable and collaborative solutions.

01Children’s residential care02Responsible capital03Property and infrastructure04Systemic change

01Area of work

Children’s residential care

Exploring better, safer and more sustainable approaches to children’s residential care.

Through the systems view

  • Environments that are genuinely suitable for children to live in and staff to work in.
  • Operating and funding models that can sustain quality over time.
  • Accountability that serves children rather than paperwork.

02Area of work

Responsible capital

Exploring how capital can support long-term value creation while contributing to meaningful outcomes.

Through the systems view

  • Patient structures with transparent terms and realistic timeframes.
  • Return measured alongside outcomes, not instead of them.
  • The conditions that allow responsible investors to participate with confidence.

03Area of work

Property and infrastructure

Understanding property not simply as an asset, but as part of the environments and infrastructure that shape people’s lives.

Through the systems view

  • Location in relation to schools, families and communities.
  • Design and adaptation with the people who will live and work there in mind.
  • Homes treated with the seriousness we give other social infrastructure.

04Area of work

Systemic change

Bringing together different perspectives, including lived experience, capital, operations and policy, to explore better solutions to complex challenges.

Through the systems view

  • Convening people who do not usually share a room.
  • Translating between the language of experience, finance, delivery and regulation.
  • Practical proposals rather than critique alone.
