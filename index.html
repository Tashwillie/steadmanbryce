01The story

I have experienced the systems I now work to change.

My journey has taken me from lived experience of the care system into entrepreneurship, property, investment and public life.

Along the way, I have seen how systems shape people’s lives: from the perspective of someone affected by them, someone working within business, and someone involved in policy and decision-making.

Today, I bring those perspectives together.

Because better outcomes require more than good intentions. They require better systems.

03The work

Complex problems cannot be solved from one perspective.

My work sits at the intersection of care, capital, property and public policy.

I am particularly interested in how responsible investment, suitable environments, strong operations and informed decision-making can contribute to better, safer and more sustainable models within children’s residential care.

The challenge is not simply to identify where systems are failing.

It is to understand how different people, resources and institutions can work together to build something better.
