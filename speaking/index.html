Better systems. Better outcomes.

Let’s talk

Speaking & media

Conversations that connect different worlds.

Louis Stedman-Bryce brings together lived experience and professional experience across business, property, investment and public life.

His perspective is particularly relevant to conversations about how different sectors can work together to create better systems and outcomes.

01Speaking topics

What Louis speaks about.

Each topic can be shaped to the audience, from investor forums to policy roundtables to frontline leadership groups.

  1. 01

    Better systems. Better outcomes.

    Why good intentions are not enough, and what it takes to design, fund, operate and govern systems that serve the people inside them.

  2. 02

    Lived experience and leadership

    How lived experience becomes a leadership asset when it sits alongside commercial, operational and policy judgement rather than in place of it.

  3. 03

    Responsible capital

    What separates capital that builds long-term value and better outcomes from capital that extracts, and how to create the conditions for the former.

  4. 04

    Property as social infrastructure

    Why the buildings people live in are care decisions as much as asset decisions, and what changes when we treat them that way.

  5. 05

    The gap between policy and reality

    How rules written in daylight play out in real homes and real organisations, and how to close the distance between the two.

  6. 06

    Entrepreneurship and systemic change

    Building ventures that are commercially sound and genuinely useful, and why the two are not in tension.
